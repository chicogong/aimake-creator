# Desk lighting shoot plan

These are observation prompts for an illustrative sample. Use your own location, setup and footage when testing a product.

## Hand shadow

Write a line while seated normally. Repeat with the lamp at another position. Compare where the shadow falls around the pen.

## Screen reflection

Check for a reflected light from the usual sitting position. Turn or move the lamp once and look again.

## Comparison footage

Hold the camera position, page, seat and exposure steady between frames. Record the one setting or position you changed.
