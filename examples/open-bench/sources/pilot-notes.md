# Open Bench pilot notes

- The desk received 72 borrow requests in the first seven days.
- Members picked up 49 tools in that period.
- The desk recorded 32 returns in that period.

## Lending desk procedure

- A member requests a tool and a pickup time.
- A volunteer confirms the pickup and sends a reminder the day before.
- The member and volunteer record the tool's condition at checkout.
- The desk checks the tool at return and records any follow-up.

## Week two plan

- Open an additional Saturday afternoon pickup window.
- Send a reminder the evening before each appointment.

This is a fictional workshop example. The quantities illustrate a reporting layout.
